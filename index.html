Privacy Policy — Beadline: Daily Logic Puzzle

Last updated: September 12, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information Beadline: Daily Logic Puzzle ("the App", "we") collects, why, and how it is used. The App is a single-player logic puzzle game with an optional daily challenge and leaderboard.

Information we collect

  • Anonymous account identifier. On first launch, the App creates an anonymous account (via our backend provider, Supabase) so your progress, streaks, and daily results can sync if you reinstall or play on another device. No email, phone number, or real name is required to create this account.
  • Nickname. A random nickname is generated automatically (e.g. "Blue Bead 4821"); you can change it. It is shown publicly on the leaderboard.
  • Country (optional). Derived from your device's locale, used only to show a country-filtered leaderboard. You can hide this in Settings at any time.
  • Gameplay data. Levels solved, time taken, hints used, streaks, and daily puzzle results, so we can show your statistics and rank you on the leaderboard.
  • Google Sign-In (optional). If you choose to link a Google account, we use it only to associate your existing anonymous progress with that account so it can be restored later. We do not store your email address in our application database.
  • Purchases. If you buy an in-app product (e.g. remove ads, hint packs, premium), the purchase is processed by Google Play Billing. We store only which products you own, not your payment details.
  • Advertising data. The App shows ads via Google AdMob, which may collect your advertising identifier and other data to show and measure ads, subject to your consent choices (a consent form is shown where required, e.g. in the EU/UK).

Third parties we use

  • Supabase — hosts our backend (authentication, database, daily puzzle results, leaderboard).
  • Google AdMob — serves in-app advertising.
  • Google Play Billing — processes in-app purchases.
  • Google Sign-In — optional account linking.

Each of these providers has its own privacy policy governing how it handles data on our behalf.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

Data retention and deletion

Gameplay data is retained as long as your account exists. To request deletion of your account and associated data, contact us at the email below.

Your choices

  • You can hide your country from the leaderboard at any time in Settings.
  • You can decline personalized ads via the consent form shown on first launch (where applicable) or your device's ad settings.
  • You can request deletion of your data by contacting us.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated date.
